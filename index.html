3RD FLOOR // INTELLIGENT DECISION

ORB15-long, $750 doctrine, one trade per day. Convert a candidate into a ticket or veto it.

Awaiting compute…

Shares = min(floor(2% equity ÷ stop-distance), floor(equity ÷ entry)), whole shares. Skip if stop > 3% of price. Target = entry + 1.5R. Long only — cash account, no locates.

  • Trigger: 5-min close above OR high AND above session VWAP. No touch entries.
  • Stop is OR low — structural, never moved down.
  • Time stop 15:55 ET. No overnight. TIF day on the bracket.
  • PDT: one round-trip per day by design. Fits cash (T+1) or margin (3/5).
  • Losing day → next day observation-only. Equity < $500 → halt.
  • Weekly drawdown > 6% → stand down until Monday.

PLAYBOOK

Doctrinal floors from TRADING VAULT · TRADING OPERATION

F1 Ingest

Every day starts here. 09:00 sweep, 09:45 opening-range capture, 16:10 archive. Feature table (gap, rvol, score, OR levels) feeds Floor 2.

F2 Analyze

score = |gap%| + OR RVOL. ≥ 2.0 to even be a candidate. Rank; only top 1 advances. Most days: nothing qualifies.

F3 Decide

ORB15-long. Entry after close-confirmation. Stop = OR low. Target 1.5R. Size off 2% equity. Cooling-off after a loss.

F4 Execute

Dry run → --approve paper → live only with dual confirm. Journal every trade. Bracket or nothing.
